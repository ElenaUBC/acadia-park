Good to know

Who can come?

Acadia Park residents, their families and guests.

Do I have to come both evenings?

No. Come to either evening, or both!

Is there a cost?

No, the Gallery is free.

When can I drop off my artwork?

We'll email everyone who submits art with drop-off and pick-up times.

Accessibility

If you need anything to take part comfortably, email e.kolmogorov@ubc.ca.

Photos

We won't be taking photos at the event. If you take your own, please be mindful of others: some neighbours may prefer not to be photographed, so ask before including people in your pictures.
